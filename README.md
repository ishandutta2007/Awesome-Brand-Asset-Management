# Awesome-Brand-Asset-Management

## Top Brand Asset Management Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Digital Asset Management, Brand Consistency & Creative Workflows*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Brand Asset Management (BAM)** and **Digital Asset Management (DAM)**. These tools help organizations store, organize, search, share, and distribute brand assets like logos, images, videos, documents, and brand guidelines to ensure consistency across teams and channels.



**Examples** include Frontify, Bynder, Brandfolder, Canto, MediaValet, Acquia DAM, Aprimo, MarcomCentral, IntelligenceBank, and Filecamp (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is expanded with active projects for self-hosting, custom asset workflows, and transparent brand data management — ideal for marketing teams, creative agencies, and developers building vendor-independent brand asset solutions. Note that while several mature open-source DAM platforms exist, the ecosystem is more fragmented than in some other categories, with many solutions being general-purpose file managers or photo organizers that require customization for enterprise brand asset management.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Frontify](https://www.frontify.com/)**  

  Brand management platform combining DAM with brand guidelines, design templates, and collaboration tools for distributed teams.



- **[Bynder](https://www.bynder.com/)**  

  Enterprise DAM with brand templating, creative workflow automation, and analytics. Offers a local Docker version for self-hosting with license .



- **[Brandfolder](https://brandfolder.com/)**  

  Digital asset management platform with AI-powered search, brand portals, and integrations for marketing workflows.



- **[Canto](https://www.canto.com/)**  

  DAM platform focused on ease of use, with facial recognition, custom portals, and metadata management for brand assets.



- **[MediaValet](https://www.mediavalet.com/)**  

  Cloud-native DAM built for enterprise with AI, workflow automation, and brand compliance tools.



- **[Acquia DAM](https://www.acquia.com/products/dam)**  

  Enterprise DAM integrated with Drupal, offering brand portals, asset delivery, and marketing workflow tools.



- **[Aprimo](https://www.aprimo.com/)**  

  Integrated marketing operations platform with DAM, content operations, and performance analytics.



- **[MarcomCentral](https://marcomcentral.com/)**  

  Brand asset management and marketing asset management for enterprise brand consistency.



- **[IntelligenceBank](https://www.intelligencebank.com/)**  

  DAM with marketing compliance, brand portals, and creative workflow management.



- **[Filecamp](https://filecamp.com/)**  

  Digital asset management for smaller teams and agencies, with brand guidelines, file sharing, and custom portals.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[ResourceSpace](https://github.com/resourcespace/resourcespace)**  

  The most widely adopted open-source DAM, with a strong focus on asset organization and sharing. Organize, manage, and share brand assets like logos, images, videos, and guidelines. Mature platform with 250,000+ users including Coca-Cola, Google, and Toyota. Web and mobile access, multilingual, 24/7 availability, unlimited users. PHP/MySQL-based .



- **[Pimcore (Community Edition)](https://github.com/pimcore/pimcore)**  

  Open-source platform for Product Information Management (PIM), Master Data Management (MDM), and Digital Asset Management (DAM). Can manage brand assets alongside product data. Gartner "Cool Vendor" recipient. Free without license costs, supports unlimited file formats and data volumes .



- **[hyper Content & Digital Asset Management Server](https://github.com/hyperCMS/hyperCMS)**  

  Open-source ready-to-use solution helping organizations keep full control over digital assets to automate processes and reduce costs. Covers brand and marketing asset management, product content management, customer experience, business automation, and enterprise content management .



- **[Phraseanet](https://github.com/alchemy-fr/Phraseanet)**  

  Open-source DAM solution for photos, videos, audio, and documents. Features Elasticsearch engine, AI-assisted batch indexing, thesaurus, metadata read/write (IPTC, XMP, DC, ID, MP3), video chaptering and subtitling (manual/AI), rights management, workflows, web galleries, sharing, publishing, and plugins for WordPress and Drupal .



- **[FocusOPEN Digital Asset Manager](https://github.com/mindworx/focusopen)**  

  Web-based open-source software for digital asset management and cataloging. Proposed as the best choice for a museum digitization pilot project at Bar County Museum, hosted on University of Belgrade's web server. Analyzed for characteristics and trade-offs of different open-source DAM types .



- **[WoodWing Assets (Elvis DAM)](https://github.com/WoodWing/assets)**  

  Flexible and scalable DAM system helping publishers, creative agencies, and marketing services achieve quality, cost-effectiveness, and time-to-market goals. Handles storage, organization, access, sharing, and rights/permissions for rich media assets. Premium Adobe InDesign integration. Available cloud and on-premises .



- **[open-brandkit](https://github.com/open-brandkit/open-brandkit)**  

  Source-available tool that turns existing brand files into a polished, shareable brand kit page for your website. Generates logo lockups, wordmarks, icons, brand color cards, avatar/favicon generation, social banner images, print-styled PDF flow, ZIP downloads, and a generated manifest. Works with Next.js 14+ App Router and Tailwind CSS. Currently early alpha .



- **[brand-assets-ecosystem](https://github.com/b-ciq/brand-assets-ecosystem)**  

  Multi-interface brand asset management system with enhanced search, metadata, and deployment capabilities. Includes web GUI (Next.js), MCP server, and shared SDK. Features asset browsing, search, download, MCP integration for both local and cloud endpoints. In development: enhanced metadata, improved search, PDF previews, Slack integration .



- **[@mcptoolshop/brand](https://github.com/mcp-tool-shop-org/brand)**  

  Centralized brand asset registry for GitHub organizations. One repo holds every logo; every README points to it via raw.githubusercontent.com URLs. Solves duplication, drift, and inconsistency across 100+ repos. Features CLI for verify, manifest, stats, audit, and migrate operations. Daily GitHub Action auto-syncs new or changed logos from org repos, regenerates manifest with SHA-256 hashes, and opens PRs .



- **[Craft Digital Asset Library Template](https://github.com/craftdocs/digital-asset-library)**  

  Free, open-source (MIT-licensed) React + TypeScript template for uploading and organizing brand assets like logos and graphics. Tracks filename, asset type, project, and upload date. Connects to Craft workspace as backend — no database needed. Users sign in with their own Craft account, data stays in private workspaces .



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **AtroDAM** — Free-forever DAM plan with asset categorization, library, sharing, metadata management, search/filter, version control, and workflow management features. SaaS/web with mobile and desktop support .

- **TagSpaces** — Open-source file organizer and browser for managing local files with tags, colors, and descriptions. Not a full DAM, but useful for personal or small-team brand asset organization .

- **DigiKam** — Open-source photo management tool with tagging, facial recognition, and organization features. Desktop-based (Windows/Mac/Linux). Better suited for photo libraries than enterprise brand assets .

- **Immich** — Self-hosted photo and video backup solution. Requires self-hosting knowledge. Mobile apps for iOS and Android. Focused on personal media management rather than brand asset workflows .

- **PhotoPrism** — Self-hosted photo management with AI-powered tagging and search. User experiences note duplicate detection and preview issues during initial import .

- **Filecamp** — While primarily SaaS, offers a free tier for smaller teams and agencies seeking brand guidelines and file sharing capabilities.



**Frameworks for building custom brand asset solutions**: For enterprise-grade open-source DAM, **ResourceSpace** provides the most mature foundation with proven scalability and brand asset management capabilities . For organizations needing integrated PIM/MDM/DAM, **Pimcore Community Edition** offers broader data management beyond assets alone . For development teams wanting a lightweight, code-first brand kit generator, **open-brandkit** integrates with Next.js/Tailwind projects . For GitHub-native brand asset consistency, **@mcptoolshop/brand** solves org-wide logo duplication . Note that true enterprise brand asset management with approval workflows, creative automation, and marketing integrations remains largely commercial territory; open-source stacks provide strong asset organization and sharing foundations that require customization for full brand operations.
